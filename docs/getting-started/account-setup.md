# Account Setup

> **한국어**: [계정 설정](/ko/docs/getting-started/account-setup)

## Register

1. Open [kvid.ai/register](https://kvid.ai/register).
2. Fill in:
   - **Username** — any non-empty handle
   - **Email** — must be a real address; you'll receive a verification email
   - **Password** — 8+ characters, including uppercase, lowercase, and a digit
   - **Confirm password**
   - Accept the Terms of Service
3. Click **Register**.

## Verify your email

- You cannot log in until your email is verified.
- Check your inbox for a message from kvidAI and click the verification link.
- No email? Open [kvid.ai/auth/resend-verification](https://kvid.ai/auth/resend-verification) and enter your address.

After verification you'll land on a confirmation page and can proceed to sign in.

## Sign in

1. Go to [kvid.ai/login](https://kvid.ai/login).
2. Enter your email (or username) and password.
3. You'll be redirected to the dashboard at [kvid.ai/dashboard](https://kvid.ai/dashboard).

## Forgot password

- Open [kvid.ai/auth/forgot-password](https://kvid.ai/auth/forgot-password).
- Enter your email; you'll receive a password-reset link.

- [Buy Credits →](./buy-credits) to enable video/image/text generation.
- If you plan to call the API from code, [create an API key](./api-keys).
