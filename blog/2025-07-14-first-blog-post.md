## What is kvidAI?

**kvidAI** is an AI generation platform specialized for K-pop and K-beauty content. We offer:

- **Video Generation** — Text-to-Video and Image-to-Video powered by the latest AI models
- **Image Generation** — High-quality images from text prompts
- **Web App** — Storyboard builder, video editor, voice library, and gallery — all in one place
- **REST API** — Integrate AI generation directly into your products via `api.kvid.ai`

## Credit-Based, Simple Pricing

All features run on a single credit balance. Buy credits once and use them across every API and every web app feature. No subscriptions required — pay only for what you generate.
